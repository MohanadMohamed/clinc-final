# Clinic terminology

Shared language for the Arabic single-doctor Neurology & Psychiatry clinic. Scope and permissions are defined in the [MVP](docs/requirements/MVP.md).

## Language

**Patient (مريض)**: A person registered with the clinic, with one continuing identity across appointments and visits.
_Avoid_: Using appointment or visit to mean the patient record.

**Appointment (موعد)**: A reserved time for a patient to attend the clinic; it is not evidence that a consultation occurred.
_Avoid_: Visit when referring to a booking.

**Visit (زيارة)**: One actual clinical encounter with the doctor, whether scheduled or a walk-in.
_Avoid_: Appointment when referring to clinical documentation.

**Clinical note (ملاحظة سريرية)**: The doctor's record of assessment and care during a visit, including sensitive neurological or psychiatric information.

**Invoice (فاتورة)**: A record of charges owed for clinic services, separate from money received.
_Avoid_: Payment when referring to a charge.

**Payment (دفعة)**: Money received toward an invoice.
_Avoid_: Invoice when referring to receipt of money.

**Receipt (إيصال)**: Evidence of a recorded payment; reprinting it does not create another payment.

**Receptionist (موظف الاستقبال)**: The clinic worker handling registration, scheduling and authorized payment administration, without access to the doctor's clinical record.
